Muscle Target

📈 Weekly Progression

If you do the same thing every week, your muscles stop growing. Follow this to force growth.

Weeks 1 - 2

The Foundation

Follow the exact reps listed in the workout plan. Focus 100% on proper form. If it says 15 reps, stop at 15. Rest 60 seconds between sets.

Weeks 3 - 4

The Overload

Add 2 to 5 reps to every exercise. (e.g., If you did 15 pushups, aim for 17-20). For planks, add 15 seconds to your hold time.

Weeks 5 - 6

The Volume Spike

Keep the higher reps from Week 3, but add a 4th set to your main exercises (Pushups, Squats, Lunges). Reduce rest time to 45 seconds.

🛒 Budget Grocery Guide

The cheapest, easiest-to-find foods to build your 6-pack.

High Protein (Muscle Builders)

  • Soya Chunks: Cheapest protein available. Great in rice/curries.
  • Eggs: Buy in trays. Boil or scramble.
  • Dal/Lentils: Moong, Toor, Chana. High protein & fiber.
  • Peanut Butter: High calories & protein for cheap.

High Carb (Energy Fuels)

  • Bananas: Perfect pre-workout fuel.
  • White/Brown Rice: Easy to digest, cheap bulk carbs.
  • Oats: Cheap breakfast carb, keeps you full.
  • Potatoes/Sweet Potatoes: Excellent post-workout recovery.
